Head-to-head comparisons and the random-calendar test

The random-calendar test re-runs each comparison on the same prices with the moon (and signs) slid forward by random amounts, or the Mercury / eclipse calendar shifted. If the real calendar barely beats the fake ones, the pattern is what luck produces. With this many tests, a few will look good by accident.
