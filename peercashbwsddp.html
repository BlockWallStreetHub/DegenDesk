Good to know

  • This page uses the open-source Peer Cash SDK (ZKP2P protocol, MIT license). Orders run on the Peer protocol on Base, not on Block Wall Street.
  • It only cashes out Base USDC. Bridge or swap to USDC on Base first, then come back.
  • Wise, PayPal and Alipay need an identity step that only Peer’s own site can do, so they are not listed here. Use peer.xyz for those.
  • Your wallet pays a small network fee (gas) in ETH on Base for each step. Peer sets any protocol fees. Block Wall Street adds no fee.
  • Where it works, and any identity checks, depend on Peer and on the payment app you choose. Rules differ by country. You are responsible for your own taxes and local laws.
  • If a transaction looks stuck, do not send it again. Press Refresh first and check your orders.
  • Never share your seed phrase or private key with anyone, including anyone claiming to be Block Wall Street.
