Derive Trade Simulator

Build any BTC options or perp trade the way you would on Derive, then see what happens at expiry and before it. Pick a starting strategy, edit the legs, and move the price, time and volatility sliders. Everything is a model estimate: replace the auto prices with the real quotes from Derive before you rely on a number.

Market

Spot, IV and fees are inputs. Use Derive's quoted IV and live price for accurate results.

Strategy

Leave "Entry price" blank to use the model price at today's spot, IV and expiry. Puts further out of the money usually trade at a higher IV (skew), so give each leg its own IV to match Derive's chain. Type the real Derive fill price to override it. For a perp leg, the strike column is your entry price and Buy means long, Sell means short.

Reading the Result

Educational simulator, not financial advice and not an offer to trade. Prices come from a Black-Scholes model (no interest rate, one IV for all strikes), so real Derive quotes will differ because of skew, spreads and liquidity. Fees are charged on open only and are editable; funding, liquidation mechanics, settlement and Derive's portfolio margin rules are not modeled. Perp liquidation is an approximation. Check every number against the live Derive order screen.
