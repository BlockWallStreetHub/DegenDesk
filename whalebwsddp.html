How the scanner works

It asks GeckoTerminal for the hottest trading pools on the network you pick, then pulls each pool's recent trades and keeps only buys of your minimum size or more in one trade. Bitcoin look-alikes (anything with BTC in the name) and stablecoins are always dropped. Every buy it sees is saved on this device, so the list builds up and survives a refresh.

The wallet shown is the address that sent the trade. For trades routed through a bot, aggregator or exchange this can be a router and not a person, so treat a wallet as a lead to check, not proof of who is behind it. Free data has a rate limit, so a scan takes roughly 2 seconds per pool.

What it can read

Ethereum-style wallets (0x…) are read on Ethereum, Base, Optimism, Arbitrum and Polygon through Blockscout. Solana wallets are read from a public Solana node and priced with DexScreener. Coins with no market price are counted but not valued. Wallets on BNB Chain and Avalanche are not covered yet.

Every refresh is compared with the one before it, so you see new positions, sold-out positions and big changes in size. Your list is saved in this browser and, if you are signed in on the Account page, backed up to your account.
