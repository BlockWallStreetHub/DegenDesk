Big-buy alerts

or more, on every coin in my list. The alert includes the buyer's wallet address.
Buys are read from GeckoTerminal's free trade feed, one coin at a time, so a long list takes a minute or two per pass. The first pass on a new coin only learns what already happened and sends nothing. After that, each new buy that size or bigger sends one alert. Works while this page is open. The wallet shown is the address that sent the trade, which can be a bot or router.
