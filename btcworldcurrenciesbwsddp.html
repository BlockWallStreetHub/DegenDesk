Data sources and settings
Live: Coinbase WebSocket (BTC-USD ticks) plus CoinGecko and CryptoCompare polling every 30 seconds for the other currencies.
History: CryptoCompare daily closes of BTC in each currency, back to Jan 2014 where available. If a currency is not available there, the tool builds it from BTC/USD (CryptoCompare or Blockchain.com) multiplied by daily ECB exchange rates from Frankfurter, and labels it "USD x FX".
