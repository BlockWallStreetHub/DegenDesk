Call-Outs PAID MEMBERS

Possible buys sorted by how long you might hold them: Scalp (hours), Swing (days to weeks) and Long-term (months). Each call-out blends chart signals (EMA, MACD, RSI, volume, volatility), whale buys from the Whale Watch tape, market momentum and trending status into one 0 to 100 score. Closed candles only. It also covers stocks, ETFs and tokenized stocks from the ticker list in Settings. A call-out is a checklist, not a promise.

Settings

Call-outs

Education, not advice. A call-out means several indicators lined up, not that price will rise. Stops can be skipped by fast markets, fees and slippage are not included, and small DEX tokens can lose most of their value in minutes (rug pulls, honeypots, thin liquidity). The safety filters reduce that risk but cannot remove it. Never put in money you cannot afford to lose, and test small first.

How the scores work
  • Scalp (hold hours): chart score from the 1-hour (60%) and 4-hour (40%) charts using the Scalp Scanner rules, then blended: chart 65%, whale buys 25%, market momentum 10%.
  • Swing (hold days to weeks): the daily chart (20 and 50 EMA trend, daily MACD, RSI zone, breakout or pullback to the 20 EMA, volume). A weak 4-hour chart takes points off. Blend: chart 55%, whales 20%, momentum 15%, trending 10%.
  • Long-term (hold months, majors, large stocks and ETFs): weekly and daily trend (200 EMA, 50-week EMA), weekly MACD and RSI, market-cap rank, distance below the all-time high, volume for its size and strength against Bitcoin over 90 days. Blend: chart 60%, quality 40%. No stop-loss trade plan: you get a scale-in zone and a line that would show the long-term trend has broken.
  • Missing data is not a penalty. If a piece (say whale buys for a major coin) is not available, the score is built from what is, and the card says what was used.
  • Whale buys come from the Whale Watch tape saved in this browser. They only exist for DEX tokens. Press Refresh whale buys, or run a scan on the Whale Watch page, to update it. Whale data is a snapshot of what your browser has collected, not the whole market.
  • DEX safety filters (all must pass, or the token is listed under Filtered out): liquidity $75,000+, pool at least 3 days old, 24h volume $50,000+, at least 3 different whale wallets, no single wallet over 60% of whale buying, biggest buy under 15% of liquidity, sells not above 1.5x buys, and top-10 holders under 60% when that data exists. DEX tokens only appear in Scalp and Swing and are always marked high risk.
  • Options (Derive): only BTC and ETH, only when the daily chart has a clear direction (score at your minimum and price above the 50 EMA for a call, score 30 or lower and price below it for a put). It picks a call or put with delta near 0.35 on an expiry about 2 to 4 and 5 to 8 weeks out, and only if the quote is liquid. You see the cost, break-even, time decay and what it would be worth at the chart target. Buying only: no selling, no spreads, no leverage.
  • History check: for majors the same rule is replayed on the candles loaded and shown on the card. A short history is a small sample.
  • Labels: 75+ High, 60+ Call-out, 50+ Watch. They describe how many boxes are ticked, not how likely it is to work.
Prices: Binance public data (CryptoCompare backup). Market data and trending: CoinGecko. DEX pools, candles and holder data: GeckoTerminal. Options quotes: Derive public market data. Stocks and ETFs: Yahoo Finance chart data; if Yahoo cannot be reached from your browser, a tokenized version (such as TSLAx or TSLAon) is looked up on a DEX through GeckoTerminal and marked on the card. Stocks have no whale-buy or trending data and no market-cap rank, so their scores are built from the chart alone and the card says so. Stock candles exist only while the market is open. Tokenized stocks have too little weekly history for a Long-term call-out and trade thinly. Prices can be delayed.
