Lesson: BTC vs Housing

Tool: Housing vs BTC (housingbtcbwsddp.html) · about 8 minutes

WHAT THIS TOOL ANSWERS

Your biggest purchase is usually a home. Your biggest question as a Bitcoin holder should be: what is that home costing me in BTC? This tool puts 15 years of daily Bitcoin prices next to the Case-Shiller home price index for major U.S. metros, so you can see both in one picture.

READ THE TOOL IN 5 STEPS

  1. Pick your range. 15Y shows everything. 1Y and 3Y zoom in on recent action.
  2. Pick markets. Click city chips to add or remove lines. Bitcoin is the orange line.
  3. Growth view. Every line starts at 100. A line at 200 doubled; a line at 100,000 grew a thousand-fold.
  4. Home priced in BTC view. Home index divided by the BTC price. Falling line = homes got cheaper when measured in Bitcoin.
  5. Scorecard and calculator. Total change, per-year change, worst drawdown, and what a home of your value would have cost in BTC on past dates.

WHY LOG SCALE IS ON

Bitcoin went up thousands of times; homes went up by a few multiples. On a normal (linear) chart the housing lines look flat against the floor. On a log scale, equal vertical distance means equal percentage change, so you can compare slow and fast growers honestly. Flip to Linear any time to see the difference.

KNOW THE LIMITS

  • Bitcoin prices daily. Housing is monthly and arrives about two months late. The tool blends between monthly points and stops at the latest print. It is not a true daily home price.
  • Case-Shiller tracks repeat sales of single-family homes in a metro. It is a market gauge, not the value of your house.
  • Past results do not predict future results. This is education, not financial advice.
