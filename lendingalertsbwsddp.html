DEGEN DESK · LENDING ALERT RULES

ALERTS YOU SET

Nothing is on until you turn it on. Every rule has two switches: On (show it on the Desk) and Email (also send it to your inbox). A rule with Email off never emails you.

Email settings

Emails go only for rules where you switched Email on. One email per check, never one per alert, and a daily cap protects your inbox.

TEST BENCH

Try your rules on sample accounts before you trust them. Change the numbers, then run a check.

How this connects to the Lending Account Manager. The rules engine is plain JavaScript: LendingAlerts.run(accounts, market) takes your accounts (health factor, 24h low, collateral, debt) and the market state (daily, weekly and monthly close versus the 20 EMA), and returns the alerts you set. Your Desk passes in its real data. The page checks only while it is open. For checks that run while your laptop is closed, the same rules need to run on a server schedule.

Swap windows are rule-based signals from a checklist you choose, not predictions and not financial advice. Nobody can promise the best time to swap. Swaps cost fees, and moving debt into a volatile coin raises your risk.

Emails use your Web3Forms key, so they arrive in the Block Wall Street inbox.
