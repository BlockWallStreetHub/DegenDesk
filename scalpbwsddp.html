How the score works
  • Trend, 30 points: 5 EMA on the right side of the 21 EMA, price beyond both, a fresh cross in the last 3 candles, and a 21 EMA sloping your way.
  • MACD momentum, 25: histogram on the right side of zero, building in your direction, and a fresh line cross.
  • RSI, 15: rewards a rising RSI above 50 for longs (or falling below 50 for shorts) and bounces out of oversold or overbought, and gives nothing when RSI is already stretched.
  • Volume, 20: the latest closed candle against its 20-candle average, halved if the candle went the other way.
  • Volatility, 10: ATR as a share of price. Too quiet to scalp scores zero and is labeled Quiet; extreme swings score low.
  • Levels: entry is the close. The stop sits just past the last 5 candles' swing, kept between 0.8 and 2.5 ATR. Targets are 1.5 and 2.5 times the risk.
  • Same indicators, every timeframe: the 5 and 21 EMA are measured in candles of that chart (5 and 21 hours on 1h, 5 and 21 four-hour candles on 4h, 5 and 21 days on daily).
  • History check: the scanner replays the same rule across the candles it loaded and counts how often price reached Target 1 before the stop. A candle that touches both counts as a loss. A short history is a small sample, so treat it as a sanity check, not a promise.
Prices come from Binance public data, with CryptoCompare as a backup. Contract-address tokens come from GeckoTerminal. Top-100 list from CoinGecko, with stablecoins and wrapped copies left out. Stocks and ETFs: Yahoo Finance chart data. If Yahoo cannot be reached from your browser, the scanner looks for a tokenized version (such as TSLAx or TSLAon) on a DEX through GeckoTerminal and marks it. Stock candles exist only while the market is open, so on nights and weekends the newest candle is from the last session. 4-hour stock candles join four consecutive 1-hour candles. The daily candle appears the morning after it closes. Prices can be delayed. Tokenized stocks trade thinly, so spreads and slippage can be large.
