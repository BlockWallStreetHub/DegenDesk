How the meter decides

The needle runs from Close through Rebalance to Hold. It starts at the middle and moves with: the 7 and 30 day trend and where price sits against its 20 and 50 day averages, how choppy or calm the last two weeks were, whether you are in range and how near an edge you are, and, when you are out of range, the estimated chance price walks back into your range within 14 days (from the coin's own recent volatility).

In short: a coin that is strongly bearish pushes toward Close because impermanent loss and the falling price compound. A calm, sideways or rising market pushes toward Hold, and if you are only a little out of range, the odds of returning decide whether to wait or re-center. Rebalancing costs gas and locks in the loss you are sitting on, so the meter only recommends it when price is unlikely to come back soon.

Impermanent loss figures are measured from the current price to your range edges, and they do not include fees you have earned. This is an educational rules-based read of past prices, not financial advice, and it cannot see news. I am not a licensed advisor.
